01

The following plans are auto-renewable subscriptions when they are offered in the App Store:

Basic Member — monthly

Basic Member — yearly

Pro Member — monthly

Pro Member — yearly

Basic lifetime (com.readio.basic.lifetime) and Pro lifetime (com.readio.pro.lifetime) are one-time purchases. They are not auto-renewable subscriptions and this renewal agreement does not make them renew.

The price is the price displayed by the App Store for your storefront before you confirm payment. This agreement does not publish a substitute price. Taxes, currency, and any introductory offer are also shown by Apple.

02

How renewal billing works续费如何扣款

After you confirm an auto-renewable plan, the payment is charged to your Apple Account, which Apple may show as an iTunes or App Store account. The subscription then renews for the same plan length unless you cancel renewal.

Apple charges the Apple Account within 24 hours before the current period ends. If that charge succeeds, the subscription continues for one more period of the same length. If Apple changes the displayed renewal price, Apple’s confirmation and applicable rules control when the new price applies.

A failed or reversed charge can interrupt renewal. Access after a billing retry, grace period, refund, or revocation follows the status Apple reports. We do not independently collect the subscription payment.

你确认自动续期方案后，费用记入 Apple 账户；Apple 也可能将其显示为 iTunes 或 App Store 账户。除非你取消续订，订阅会按相同方案时长续订。

Apple 会在当前周期结束前 24 小时内向 Apple 账户扣费。扣费成功后，订阅顺延一个相同长度的周期。若 Apple 变更所展示的续订价格，新价格何时生效以 Apple 的确认和适用规则为准。

扣费失败或被撤销可能中断续订。账单重试、宽限期、退款或撤销之后的访问权限，以 Apple 报告的状态为准。我们不另行收取订阅款项。

03

How to cancel renewal如何取消续订

You can cancel at any time, effective at the end of the current paid period. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Cancellation is confirmed by Apple, not by deleting the App.

  • On iPhone or iPad, open Settings.
  • Tap your name, or open your Apple Account. Older system versions may show this under iTunes & App Store or App Store.
  • Tap Subscriptions. You can also open Apple subscription management.
  • Select the Readio plan and turn off auto-renewal, or follow the cancel control Apple displays.

After renewal is turned off, you normally keep the membership benefits through the end of the period already paid, unless Apple refunds or revokes the transaction. Canceling does not delete local books, voice samples, or cloud copies. Remove those separately if you want them removed.

Uninstalling Readio, turning off a network connection, or revoking a third-party provider does not cancel the Apple subscription.

你可以随时取消，取消后在当前已付周期结束时生效。为避免下一次扣费，请至少在当前周期结束前 24 小时取消。取消是否成功由 Apple 确认，删除 App 不会完成取消。

  • 在 iPhone 或 iPad 上打开“设置”。
  • 点按你的姓名，或打开 Apple 账户。较旧的系统版本可能在“iTunes 与 App Store”或 App Store 中显示。
  • 点按“订阅”。也可以打开 Apple 订阅管理。
  • 选择 Readio 的方案并关闭自动续订，或按 Apple 展示的取消操作完成。

关闭续订后，通常可继续使用会员权益至已付周期结束，除非 Apple 退款或撤销该交易。取消不会删除本地书籍、声音样本或云端副本。如果希望删除，需要另行处理。

卸载爱听书、关闭网络或撤销第三方服务商授权，都不会取消 Apple 订阅。

04

Changing or restoring a plan更换或恢复方案

If you switch between monthly and yearly, or between Basic and Pro, the plan change takes effect at the time shown on the App Store confirmation. Do not rely on this page to predict a different effective date.

Buying a lifetime plan does not automatically cancel an active monthly or yearly subscription. To avoid another subscription charge, cancel renewal separately in Manage Subscriptions.

Use Restore Purchases in the App with the same Apple Account when a subscription should be active but is not shown. Restoration depends on Apple verifying the transaction. Readio does not provide a separate username and password for this purchase.

在月度与年度之间更换，或在初级与高级之间更换时，方案变更于 App Store 确认页显示的时间生效。请勿以本页推测不同的生效日期。

购买永久方案不会自动取消仍在生效的月度或年度订阅。为避免订阅再次扣费，请另外在“管理订阅”中取消续订。

订阅应当有效但 App 未显示时，请使用同一 Apple 账户在 App 内执行“恢复购买”。能否恢复取决于 Apple 是否验证该交易。Readio 不为这项购买提供单独的用户名和密码。

05

What the renewing plan provides续订方案提供什么

During a paid period, a Basic subscription provides the current Basic benefits, and a Pro subscription provides the current Pro benefits, as described in the Membership agreement. Renewal extends the same level for another period. It does not by itself upgrade Basic to Pro.

Quotas reset under the App’s current daily or weekly rules. Unused time in one period is not carried forward as a balance. Renewal does not include third-party API credit or guarantee that an external provider remains available.

在已付周期内，初级订阅提供当前的初级权益，高级订阅提供当前的高级权益，具体见 会员服务协议。续订会把同一等级再延长一个周期，本身不会把初级升级为高级。

额度按 App 当前的每日或每周规则重置。某一周期未用完的时长不会结转为余额。续订不包含第三方 API 余额，也不保证外部服务商持续可用。

A refund or revocation can end access before the original period ends. This agreement does not exclude a refund or consumer remedy that applicable law requires Apple or we must provide.

App Store 退款或扣费问题请通过 Apple 购买项目支持 提交。某笔费用是否可以退款，由 Apple 决定。Apple 已验证或变更交易后，如需协助确认当前生效的 Readio 方案，请联系 listenbook999@outlook.com。

退款或撤销可能使权益在原周期结束前终止。本协议不排除适用法律要求 Apple 或我们必须提供的退款或其他消费者救济。

Apple displays the renewal price that applies to your subscription. If that price changes, follow the notice and confirmation flow Apple provides. We do not charge a different renewal amount outside that flow.

We may update this agreement and will change its published date when we do. Material changes receive an appropriate notice. Continuing a future renewal after the required notice does not waive a mandatory right that applies to an amount you have already paid.

Apple 会展示适用于你的订阅的续订价格。价格变化时，按 Apple 提供的通知和确认流程处理。我们不会在该流程之外另收不同的续订金额。

我们可能更新本协议，并在更新后修改发布日期。重大变化会给予适当提示。在必要提示之后继续未来的续订，不代表放弃对已支付金额所适用的强制性权利。

08

End of renewal续订结束

Renewal ends when you cancel, when Apple stops billing or revokes the transaction, or when the plan is no longer available under Apple’s rules. After it ends, the App returns the affected benefits to the access level Apple’s remaining purchases support, which may be free limits or another active plan.

Ending renewal does not delete your library. Back up anything you still need and manage cloud copies separately. Existing obligations to a third-party provider remain with that provider.

当你取消、Apple 停止扣费或撤销交易，或该方案按 Apple 的规则不再提供时，续订结束。结束后，受影响的权益回到 Apple 其余购买仍支持的访问等级，可能是免费限制，也可能是另一项仍有效的方案。

结束续订不会删除书库。请另行备份仍需要的内容，并单独管理云端副本。对第三方服务商已产生的义务仍由该服务商关系处理。
