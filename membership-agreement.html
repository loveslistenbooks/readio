02

Membership plans会员方案

The App currently defines two paid levels: Basic Member (初级会员) and Pro Member (高级会员). Each level may be offered as a monthly plan, a yearly plan, or a lifetime purchase. A plan appears for purchase only when Apple returns it and the purchase screen can display it.

Basic monthly
Auto-renewable subscription. Product ID: com.readio.basic. Period: one month, until canceled.
Basic yearly
Auto-renewable subscription. Product ID: com.readio.basic1year. Period: one year, until canceled.
Basic lifetime
One-time, non-renewing purchase. Product ID: com.readio.basic.lifetime. It does not renew.
Pro monthly
Auto-renewable subscription. Product ID: com.readio.pro. Period: one month, until canceled.
Pro yearly
Auto-renewable subscription. Product ID: com.readio.pro1year. Period: one year, until canceled.
Pro lifetime
One-time, non-renewing purchase. Product ID: com.readio.pro.lifetime. It does not renew.

We do not state a fixed price in this agreement. The price, currency, tax, introductory offer, and billing period are those shown by the App Store before you confirm the purchase. If a product is unavailable, choose another displayed plan or try again later.

App 当前定义两个付费等级：初级会员（Basic Member）和高级会员（Pro Member）。每个等级可能提供月度方案、年度方案或永久购买。只有 Apple 返回商品且购买页能够展示时，该方案才可供购买。

03

What each level includes各等级包含的权益

Benefits below describe the current in-app rules. They can change with an App update. A paid change that materially affects an existing purchase is handled under the purchase terms and applicable law, not by silently treating the old purchase as canceled.

Bookshelf
Without membership, importing is limited to 5 books. Basic and Pro remove that bookshelf limit.
Sync and reading
Basic and Pro can use sync, reader backgrounds, and custom fonts. Free use does not include those features.
AI and multi-voice
Reading AI and multi-voice listening require Basic or Pro. Membership does not include a third-party model, tool, or API balance.
Voice clones
You can save 3 cloned voices without membership, 20 with Basic, and an unlimited number with Pro, subject to device storage and the rights you have in each recording.
Local clone listening
Local cloned-voice listening is limited to 1 hour per day without membership and 4 hours per day with Basic. Pro listening for this source is not limited by that daily allowance.
Network listening
Network TTS is limited to 8 hours per week without membership. Basic and Pro are not limited by that weekly allowance.
Microsoft TTS
Microsoft TTS is limited to 4 hours per week without membership. Basic and Pro are not limited by that weekly allowance. Microsoft Max TTS is unavailable without membership, limited to 4 hours per week with Basic, and not limited by that weekly allowance with Pro.
Plugin TTS
Plugin TTS is limited to 2 hours per week without membership and 8 hours per week with Basic. Pro is not limited by that weekly allowance.
Pro-only features
Sleep timer and multi-device bookshelf require Pro. Pro includes the Basic benefits unless a more specific Pro rule above says otherwise.

“Not limited by that allowance” means the App does not apply the stated membership quota. It does not promise unlimited device storage, uninterrupted providers, or that an external service will accept unlimited use. Listening time follows the App’s usage record for rendered playback.

以下权益描述的是当前 App 内规则，可能随版本更新调整。对已有购买有实质影响的付费变更，按购买约定和适用法律处理，不会把原购买默示为已取消。

书架
未开通会员时，导入上限为 5 本书。初级和高级会员不再受该书架数量限制。
同步与阅读
初级和高级会员可使用同步、阅读器背景和自定义字体。未开通会员不包含这些功能。
AI 与多语音
阅读 AI 和多语音听书需要初级或高级会员。会员不包含第三方模型、工具或 API 余额。
克隆声音
未开通会员可保存 3 个克隆声音，初级会员 20 个，高级会员数量不限；仍受设备存储空间和你对每段录音所拥有权利的限制。
本地克隆听书
本地克隆听书未开通会员为每天 1 小时，初级会员为每天 4 小时。高级会员不受该每日额度限制。
网络听书
网络 TTS 未开通会员为每周 8 小时。初级和高级会员不受该每周额度限制。
微软 TTS
微软 TTS 未开通会员为每周 4 小时。初级和高级会员不受该每周额度限制。微软 Max TTS 未开通会员不可用，初级会员每周 4 小时，高级会员不受该每周额度限制。
插件 TTS
插件 TTS 未开通会员为每周 2 小时，初级会员为每周 8 小时。高级会员不受该每周额度限制。
高级会员专属
定时停止和多端书架需要高级会员。除上面已单独说明的高级规则外，高级会员包含初级会员权益。

“不受该额度限制”指 App 不再套用上述会员配额，不代表设备存储无限、服务永不中断，也不代表外部服务接受无限制使用。听书时长按 App 对实际播放的用量记录计算。

What membership does not include会员不包含的内容

  • A catalog of books, a license to copyrighted material you do not already have rights to use, or permission to bypass another service’s access controls.
  • Prepaid credit for a third-party AI, TTS, cloud, plugin, or API provider. Those providers may bill you separately under their own terms.
  • Guaranteed offline availability. Some listening and AI features need downloaded resources, a configured provider, or a network connection.
  • A promise that generated speech, transcription, summaries, or answers are accurate, complete, or suitable for every file.
  • Automatic cancellation of another Apple subscription. Buying lifetime access does not by itself turn off an existing monthly or yearly renewal.
  • 不包含书籍内容库，不授予你原本无权使用的著作权材料，也不允许绕过其他服务的访问控制。
  • 不包含第三方 AI、TTS、云服务、插件或 API 的预付余额。这些服务商可能按各自条款另行收费。
  • 不保证全部功能离线可用。部分听书和 AI 功能需要已下载的资源、已配置的服务商或网络连接。
  • 不承诺合成语音、转写、摘要或回答准确、完整，或适用于所有文件。
  • 不会自动取消另一项 Apple 订阅。购买永久权益本身不会关闭已有的月度或年度续订。
05

When access starts, ends, or changes权益何时生效、结束或变更

Access follows Apple’s verified transaction. A subscription remains available through the paid period Apple reports, unless Apple refunds, revokes, or otherwise changes it. A lifetime purchase remains available while Apple reports that non-consumable purchase as valid.

If you already have an auto-renewing subscription and buy a lifetime plan, the lifetime purchase does not automatically cancel that subscription. Turn renewal off in Manage Subscriptions if you want to avoid another charge. If you change from one subscription plan to another, the effective time is the time shown on the App Store confirmation.

Canceling renewal does not normally remove access before the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the App, signing out of a third-party service, or stopping use does not by itself cancel an Apple subscription or refund a lifetime purchase.

Where the product supports it, use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account that made the purchase. We cannot restore a purchase that Apple does not verify, and we do not operate a separate account login for membership.

权益以 Apple 验证的交易为准。订阅在 Apple 报告的已付周期内可用，除非 Apple 退款、撤销或另行变更。永久购买在 Apple 仍将该非消耗型购买报告为有效时继续可用。

如果已有自动续期订阅又购买永久方案，永久购买不会自动取消该订阅。若不希望再次扣费，请在“管理订阅”中关闭续订。从一个订阅方案更换到另一个时，生效时间以 App Store 确认页显示的时间为准。

取消续订通常不会提前移除当前已付周期内的权益。删除 App、退出第三方服务或停止使用，本身不会取消 Apple 订阅，也不会退还永久购买的款项。

商品支持恢复时，请使用购买时的同一 Apple 账户执行“恢复购买”。Apple 未能验证的购买，我们无法恢复。会员不依赖单独的 App 账户登录。

For an App Store billing issue or refund request, use Apple’s purchase support. Apple decides eligibility under its process and applicable law. Contact listenbook999@outlook.com if a verified purchase is not reflected in the App. This agreement does not remove a mandatory refund or other consumer right.

External provider charges are not Apple membership charges. Review the provider’s price and terms before sending a book, prompt, or voice request that can incur a fee.

你确认购买后，费用记入 Apple 账户。月度与年度方案按 自动续费协议 自动续订。永久方案只收取一次费用，不会续订。

App Store 扣费问题或退款请求请通过 Apple 购买项目支持 处理。是否符合退款条件，由 Apple 按其流程和适用法律决定。已验证的购买未在 App 中生效时，请联系 listenbook999@outlook.com。本协议不排除依法必须保障的退款或其他消费者权利。

外部服务商收费不是 Apple 会员费用。发送可能产生费用的书籍、提示或语音请求前，请先查看该服务商的价格和条款。
