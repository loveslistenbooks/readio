Local-first does not mean never online. This policy separates on-device data from the information needed by cloud, voice, AI, purchase, and hosting services.本地优先，不等于从不联网。我们将本机数据与云服务、语音、AI、购买及托管服务所需的信息分开说明，帮助你做出知情选择。

01

Scope and who we are适用范围与我们的角色

This policy applies to 爱听书 / Readio (the “App”) and its official website. “We” refers to the App’s developer and maintainer. Contact us at listenbook999@outlook.com about the processing described here.

Readio is a tool for importing, managing, reading, and listening to books you are entitled to use, with optional cloud, voice, and AI features. It is not a built-in licensed book catalog. Third-party services you connect have their own privacy terms.

We explain processing by purpose below. Where applicable law requires consent, a separate choice, or permission for a particular feature, that requirement is not replaced merely by your viewing this policy.

本政策适用于爱听书 / Readio（下称“App”）及其官方网站。“我们”指 App 的开发与维护方。有关本政策所述信息处理的问题，可通过 listenbook999@outlook.com 联系我们。

爱听书是用于导入、管理、阅读和收听你有权使用的书籍的工具，并提供可选的云服务、语音与 AI 功能；它不是内置正版书籍内容库。你连接的第三方服务另有各自的隐私规则。

下文按用途说明数据处理。对于依法需要同意、单独选择或系统授权的功能，不会仅因你浏览本政策就视为已经取得相应授权。

02

Your library and on-device data书库与本机数据

To provide reading and listening, the App keeps data such as imported files, book metadata and covers, bookmarks, reading positions, reading statistics, settings, and downloaded or generated audio caches on your device. Which records exist depends on the features you use.

Voice profiles may contain reference recordings, reference text, voice parameters, and local model resources. Reading AI can create local conversations, summaries, retrieval indexes, and memory records.

Service addresses, account authorization information, and API credentials that you configure are used to connect to the corresponding services. Protect your device and any exported configuration or backup. “Stored locally” does not mean that a system backup or a sync you enable can never copy that data elsewhere.

为提供阅读与听书功能，App 会在设备上保存导入的文件、书籍信息与封面、书签、阅读位置、阅读统计、偏好设置以及下载或生成的音频缓存等数据。实际产生的记录取决于你使用的功能。

声音配置可能包含参考录音、参考文本、声音参数及本地模型资源。阅读 AI 可能生成本地对话、摘要、检索索引和记忆记录。

你配置的服务地址、账户授权信息与 API 凭证用于连接相应服务。请保护设备以及导出的配置或备份。“保存在本机”不代表系统备份或你启用的同步永远不会将相应数据复制到其他位置。

03

Cloud, imports, and online speech云服务、导入与网络语音

Files and remote sources
Importing from Files/iCloud Drive, direct URLs, WebDAV, OPDS, S3, or a supported cloud provider requires access to the selected files or service. That provider may receive authentication details, file paths, search or listing requests, and download requests. Remote or partial-book access can continue requesting content while you read.
Cloud synchronization
When you enable or run sync to iCloud, WebDAV, or S3, the sync package can include book files, titles, progress, voice settings, and, depending on the options, reference recordings and audio caches. Review the sync options before enabling it, especially those involving another person’s voice. Cloud copies follow the selected provider’s storage and access controls.
Network TTS and plugins
Online speech synthesis sends the text to be spoken and necessary voice/request settings to the engine or endpoint being used. A plugin or custom endpoint may involve its own provider. System speech and local models have different resource and network requirements; not every voice is offline.
Local Wi-Fi transfer
File transfer makes a local service available to devices that can reach it on the network. Use a trusted private network, check the address and recipient, and stop the transfer service when finished.

Check the operator, destination, privacy policy, and permissions of each service before connecting. Where you use Microsoft’s services, see Microsoft Privacy Statement. User-defined endpoints are not all operated or reviewed by us.

文件与远程来源
从“文件”/iCloud Drive、直链、WebDAV、OPDS、S3 或受支持云盘导入时，需要访问你选定的文件或服务。对应服务可能接收鉴权信息、文件路径、搜索或目录请求及下载请求。远程或部分下载的书籍在阅读过程中仍可能继续请求内容。
云同步
启用或执行 iCloud、WebDAV 或 S3 同步时，同步包可能包含书籍文件、书名、进度、声音设置，并根据选项包含参考录音与音频缓存。启用前请检查同步选项，尤其是涉及他人声音的内容。云端副本受所选服务的存储和访问规则约束。
网络 TTS 与插件
在线语音合成会将待朗读文本及必要的声音、请求设置发送给实际使用的引擎或接口。插件和自定义接口可能涉及其各自的服务商。系统语音和本地模型的资源、联网需求不同，并非所有声音都能离线使用。
局域网 Wi-Fi 传输
文件传输会向可访问该地址的局域网设备开放本地服务。请使用可信的私人网络，核对地址与接收方，并在完成后关闭传输服务。

连接前请了解对应服务的运营方、请求目标、隐私政策与权限。使用 Microsoft 服务时，可查阅 Microsoft 隐私声明。用户自定义接口并非都由我们运营或审核。

04

Reading AI and external models阅读 AI 与外部模型

When you use book questions, chapter summaries, character analysis, role identification, or similar AI features, the App may send your question, relevant book excerpts, selected context, retrieved material, memory, and necessary tool results to the AI service you configure. The scope depends on the task and your settings.

Local retrieval indexes do not by themselves mean every AI request stays local. If you enable remote embeddings, chapter excerpts may be sent to the embedding service. Enabled external tools may also contact their configured servers and receive the arguments needed for the tool call.

Use the App’s sending-scope, tool-permission, index/memory, and usage controls to limit processing. Avoid sending sensitive or unauthorized material. The selected provider’s terms determine its retention, training use, region, and deletion process; we cannot promise that every external provider follows the same rules.

使用随书问答、章节摘要、人物分析、角色识别等 AI 功能时，App 可能将你的问题、相关书籍片段、选定的上下文、检索资料、记忆及必要的工具结果发送给你配置的 AI 服务。发送范围取决于任务与设置。

本地检索索引不代表所有 AI 请求都在本机完成。启用远程向量服务时，章节片段可能被发送给对应服务；启用的外部工具也可能连接所配置的服务器，并接收调用所需参数。

你可以使用 App 中的发送范围、工具权限、索引与记忆、用量等设置限制处理。请避免发送敏感或未经授权的资料。服务商对数据的保留、训练用途、处理地区与删除方式，由其规则决定；我们不能保证所有外部服务采用相同政策。

05

Voice references and permissions参考声音与系统权限

Microphone and voice files
Microphone access is requested when recording a voice reference. You may also import a recording and its reference text. Local voice cloning uses reference material and local model resources on the device; model downloads make network requests. Recordings may leave the device if you include them in cloud sync, export, or sharing.
Speech recognition
If you ask the App to transcribe reference audio, it requests speech-recognition authorization. It requires on-device recognition where the system reports support; otherwise Apple’s recognition service may process audio over the network. Review Apple Privacy Policy and the system permission prompt.
Files and local network
File selection, cloud authorization, and local-network functions require access appropriate to the action. The operating system may ask for permission. You can refuse or revoke permissions in system settings; the associated feature may then be unavailable. Ordinary text reading does not require recording your voice.

Only supply recordings and voices you have the right and any required consent to use. A recording can contain personal or sensitive information about the speaker; do not submit someone else’s recording without appropriate authorization.

麦克风与声音文件
录制参考声音时会申请麦克风权限；你也可以导入录音及参考文本。本地声音克隆在设备上使用参考资料和本地模型资源，模型下载会产生网络请求。将录音纳入云同步、导出或分享时，录音可能离开设备。
语音识别
让 App 转写参考录音时，会申请语音识别授权。系统报告支持本地识别时，App 要求在设备上识别；否则可能由 Apple 的识别服务通过网络处理音频。请同时查看 Apple 隐私政策 与系统权限提示。
文件与本地网络
文件选择、云账户授权及局域网功能需要与该操作相应的访问权限，操作系统可能弹出授权提示。你可以拒绝或在系统设置中撤回权限，相应功能可能因此无法使用。普通文本阅读不需要录制你的声音。

请仅提供你有权使用、且已取得必要同意的录音和声音。录音可能包含说话人的个人信息或敏感信息；未经适当授权，请勿提交他人的录音。

06

Purchases and app checks购买与应用检查

App Store purchases and restores are processed through Apple’s StoreKit. The App verifies and caches entitlement information such as product and transaction identifiers, purchase/expiry dates, and revocation or refund status to determine access. Apple processes payment-account information; the App does not need your full payment-card details.

The App can automatically retrieve remote update/configuration and promotional information from configured GitLab/GitHub resources, including during its lifecycle checks. These requests expose normal connection information such as IP address, request time, and network headers to the hosting provider. The current configuration-file requests do not contain book text or reference recordings.

Other network requests, including model downloads, also reveal normal connection information to their destinations. Hosting and Apple’s processing are described in GitHub General Privacy Statement, GitLab Privacy Statement, and Apple Privacy Policy.

App Store 购买与恢复购买通过 Apple StoreKit 处理。App 会验证并缓存产品与交易标识、购买和到期时间、撤销或退款状态等权益信息，以判断功能访问权限。支付账户资料由 Apple 处理，App 不需要你的完整银行卡信息。

App 会在生命周期检查等场景下，自动从已配置的 GitLab/GitHub 资源获取更新、配置及优惠相关信息。托管方会因此接触 IP 地址、请求时间、网络请求头等通常的连接信息。当前配置文件请求中不包含书籍正文或参考录音。

模型下载等其他联网请求，也会向其目标服务暴露通常的连接信息。可分别查阅 GitHub 隐私声明、GitLab 隐私声明 与 Apple 隐私政策，了解托管及 Apple 服务的数据处理规则。

Website and email contact官网与邮件联系

This static website stores your manual language choice in your browser’s localStorage under “love-listen-books.language”. This is a preference, not a book upload. The current website does not embed third-party advertising, analytics scripts, or remote fonts. You can remove its stored preference by clearing this site’s browser data.

When the website is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub can process IP addresses and request information needed to deliver and secure the site. Its hosting logs are separate from the website’s language preference. See GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you email listenbook999@outlook.com, your email address, message, and any attachments are processed to respond and follow up. Supply only what is needed. Email providers also process the message under their own policies; do not send passwords, API keys, full payment details, or unnecessary recordings and book files.

本静态官网会使用浏览器 localStorage 中的“love-listen-books.language”记录你手动选择的语言。这是显示偏好，不是书籍上传。当前官网未嵌入第三方广告、统计追踪脚本或远程字体。清除此网站的浏览器数据即可移除该偏好。

官网部署于 GitHub Pages 时，GitHub 可能处理提供网站内容与安全保障所需的 IP 地址及请求信息。托管日志与官网保存的语言偏好是不同的数据处理。详情请见 GitHub 隐私声明。

向 listenbook999@outlook.com 发邮件时，你的邮箱地址、邮件正文及主动附上的附件会用于回复与跟进。请仅提供必要资料。邮件服务商也会依据其政策处理邮件；请勿发送密码、API 密钥、完整支付资料或不必要的录音、书籍文件。

Recipients, location, and security接收方、处理地区与安全

Data is provided to the services needed for the actions described above, to recipients you choose when exporting or sharing, and where disclosure is required by applicable law or necessary for a lawful security response. A connected provider is not automatically the App’s developer or a service we control.

Processing location depends on your device, system/cloud backups, hosting, and the service region of each provider. Some providers may process data outside your country or region. We do not promise that all data remains in one country; review the destination before connecting. Additional notices, safeguards, or consent must be provided where legally required.

We use the App’s available platform security and access controls, but no device, network, or storage is risk-free. Use trusted HTTPS endpoints where available, restrict cloud access, keep devices updated, and revoke leaked credentials. A file hash or an HTTPS connection is not a promise of end-to-end encryption for your entire cloud backup.

数据会按上述功能需要提供给相应服务，在你导出或分享时提供给你选择的接收方，以及在适用法律要求或依法处置安全事件所必要的情况下提供。连接的服务商不当然等同于 App 开发方，也不一定受我们控制。

处理地区取决于你的设备、系统或云端备份、托管服务，以及各服务商的服务区域。部分服务可能在你所在国家或地区之外处理数据。我们不承诺所有数据都留在同一个国家；连接前请检查处理目标。依法需要时，应提供额外告知、保护措施或取得相应同意。

我们使用 App 可用的平台安全与访问控制机制，但设备、网络和存储均无法做到零风险。请优先使用可信的 HTTPS 接口、限制云存储权限、及时更新设备，并撤销泄露的凭证。文件校验值或 HTTPS 连接不代表整个云备份已具备端到端加密。

Retention and removing copies保存期限与副本删除

  • Local library files and generated records remain as needed for the feature until you remove them through the available library, cache, voice, or AI management controls. Check each category: removing a book is not necessarily the same as clearing all related audio, AI, or backup data.
  • Cloud sync packages, exports, system backups, and data already sent to a provider are separate copies. Deleting a local file, disconnecting an account, or uninstalling the App does not necessarily delete those copies, revoke provider tokens, remove every system-held credential, or cancel a subscription.
  • Use your cloud provider and device backup settings to remove remote copies, and contact an AI/voice provider for data already processed by it. Retention of provider logs and purchase records follows that provider’s rules and applicable legal obligations.
  • Correspondence handled by us is retained only as needed to resolve and follow up on the request, protect legitimate rights, or meet legal obligations; we will delete or de-identify it when that need ends, subject to required retention.
  • 本地书库文件与生成记录会按功能需要保存，直到你通过可用的书库、缓存、声音或 AI 管理入口移除。请逐项检查：删除一本书不一定等于清除全部相关音频、AI 记录或备份。
  • 云同步包、导出文件、系统备份及已发送给服务商的数据属于不同副本。删除本地文件、断开账户或卸载 App，不一定会删除这些副本、撤销服务商令牌、移除全部系统保存的凭证，也不会自动取消订阅。
  • 请在云服务与设备备份设置中删除远端副本；已由 AI 或语音服务处理的数据，请按该服务的流程申请删除。服务商日志和购买记录的保存受其规则与适用法律义务约束。
  • 由我们处理的联系邮件，仅在解决与跟进请求、保护正当权益或履行法律义务所需的期限内保留；相关需要结束后，将删除或去标识化，但依法需要继续保留的除外。
10

Your choices and rights你的选择与权利

You can manage local records, stop using online engines or AI, adjust AI sending/tool settings, disconnect configured services, and withdraw system permissions. Disabling a feature stops its future use but does not undo processing already completed or necessarily stop separate app update/purchase checks.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, a copy or portability, restriction of processing, or withdrawal of consent, and may raise a privacy complaint. Email listenbook999@outlook.com with the request type and enough information to locate the relevant record. We may ask for proportionate verification, not unnecessary identity documents.

We will respond within applicable legal time limits. Where a record is held only on your device or by a provider we do not control, we will explain the relevant steps or limits rather than imply that we can directly access or delete it. Your right to contact an appropriate regulator is not restricted.

你可以管理本地记录、停用联网引擎或 AI、调整 AI 发送范围和工具权限、断开已配置服务，并撤回系统授权。停用功能会停止之后对该功能的使用，但不会撤销已完成的处理，也不一定停止独立的应用更新或购买检查。

在适用法律规定的范围内，你可以请求访问、更正、删除、获取副本或转移、限制处理、撤回同意，或提出隐私投诉。请向 listenbook999@outlook.com 说明请求类型，并提供定位相关记录所需的信息。我们可能要求适当的核验，但不会要求与请求无关的身份证明。

我们会在适用法律要求的时限内回复。对于仅存于你的设备或我们无法控制的第三方的数据，会说明操作方式或限制，不会声称能够直接访问或删除。你向适当监管机构反映问题的权利不受限制。

Children and guardians未成年人及监护人

Minors should review the use of this App, purchases, recordings, and external services with a parent or guardian. For a child under 14, or a higher age where local law requires it, obtain the guardian’s required consent before providing personal information or using features that transmit it.

Do not submit a child’s recording or other sensitive information without proper authorization. If a guardian believes that a child’s information was improperly provided to us, contact listenbook999@outlook.com so that we can assess and handle the request under applicable law.

未成年人应在父母或其他监护人指导下了解 App 使用、购买、录音与外部服务。对于未满 14 周岁儿童，或当地法律规定的更高年龄范围，在提供个人信息或使用会传输这些信息的功能前，应取得法律要求的监护人同意。

未经适当授权，请勿提交儿童录音或其他敏感信息。监护人如认为儿童信息被不当提供给我们，可通过 listenbook999@outlook.com 联系，以便依法核实和处理。
